Advanced Antioxidant & Detox.

An escalated antioxidant course — higher doses of vitamin C, α-lipoic acid and glutathione for periods of accumulated stress, sleep loss or prolonged fatigue.

The aim

What this drip is for.

A three-bag infusion. Vitamin C and higher-dose α-lipoic acid are given first, followed by glutathione in a dedicated saline bag so the master antioxidant is delivered intact. This is the R2 protocol for guests who need to reinforce antioxidant defence rather than gentle maintenance.

Time45 to 60 minutes in a private suite
InvestmentBy consultation
CourseProgramme course set by your physician; often paired with lifestyle review
Who it may suitGuests dealing with recent high stress, poor sleep, work fatigue, or those who want to reinforce antioxidant defence beyond the standard Detox Drip.

Inside the drip

The formula, ingredient by ingredient.

Each component is chosen for a specific effect. Nothing is here for weight or theatre — every line has a reason.

  • Normal saline

    Three saline lines allow each active to be delivered on its own bag for stability and pace.

  • Vitamin C

    High-dose antioxidant support delivered on its own bag.

  • Thiocan (α-lipoic acid)

    A stronger α-lipoic acid infusion for antioxidant recycling and hepatic support.

  • Glutathione

    The master antioxidant. Given in its own bag — never mixed with vitamin C or other nutrients — so the free thiol group is not oxidised before it reaches the tissues.

Clinical note: glutathione is never mixed with high-dose vitamin C or multi-nutrient bags — the free thiol group would oxidise. R2 administers it alone in a dedicated 100 ml saline bag.
