Ask about R2Exo

What exosomes are

Tiny messengers, between cells.

Exosomes are nano-sized vesicles that cells release to talk to one another. They carry proteins, peptides, growth factors and microRNA — signals that encourage repair and help calm inflammation.

R2Exo is derived from human umbilical-cord mesenchymal stem cells (UC-MSCs), valued in regenerative medicine for their high growth potential, low immunogenicity and anti-inflammatory, immunomodulatory activity. A 2024 clinical study of UC-MSC therapy in aging frailty reported lower TNF-α and IL-17 together with better quality of life and physical performance.

Zhu et al., Stem Cell Research & Therapy (2024) 15:122

Advantages of exosome treatment

What exosomes may support.

  • Skin regeneration

    Boosts fibroblast activity and collagen production — softer wrinkles, better suppleness, and less oxidative stress in aging skin.

  • Muscle regeneration

    Activates satellite cells that rebuild muscle, which may ease age-related muscle loss (sarcopenia) and improve strength.

  • Cardiovascular health

    May improve vascular function and stimulate new micro-vessels, relevant to atherosclerosis and ischaemic conditions.

  • Neurological support

    Modulates neuro-inflammation and supports neuron survival; studied in Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s and stroke recovery.

In stroke research, exosomes from Wharton’s-jelly stem cells improved functional recovery, movement and grip strength and protected cortical neurons (Cell Transplantation, 2024). These are research findings; suitability for you is decided by your physician.
