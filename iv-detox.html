Detox Drip.

Metabolic and hepatic support — a clean, focused antioxidant infusion to help the body clear its own waste.

The aim

What this drip is for.

A 250 ml saline infusion with B-complex, N-acetylcysteine and α-lipoic acid, closing with a slow glutathione IV push. This staged sequence protects the glutathione from oxidation, so the master antioxidant reaches the tissues intact.

Time45 to 60 minutes in a private suite
InvestmentBy consultation
CourseSingle session or a short course, set by your physician
Who it may suitThose looking for periodic hepatic and antioxidant support — after a heavy work period, travel, or dietary indulgence. Also a common maintenance choice between Premium Anti-Aging sessions.

Inside the drip

The formula, ingredient by ingredient.

Each component is chosen for a specific effect. Nothing is here for weight or theatre — every line has a reason.

  • Normal saline

    Balanced hydration base to carry the actives at a steady pace.

  • Vitamin B-complex

    Cofactors for energy metabolism and nervous-system support during detoxification.

  • Ancare (N-acetylcysteine)

    Precursor to glutathione — supports the liver’s Phase II detoxification pathways.

  • Thiocan (α-lipoic acid)

    Hepatoprotective antioxidant that recycles glutathione and vitamin C.

  • Glutathione

    The body’s master antioxidant. Given separately at the end of the session so the thiol group is not degraded by other nutrients in the bag.

Glutathione is administered separately from the main bag so that its thiol group is not oxidised by other nutrients.
