Apheresis · Taipei only

Therapeutic Plasma Exchange.

A physician-supervised blood-purification session on the Haemonetics MCS+ apheresis system — single needle, one arm, up to two hours.

The therapy

What this therapy is.

Plasma exchange separates the fluid part of your blood (plasma) from your cells. The plasma is discarded, sterile replacement fluid is added, and your own red and white cells are returned to circulation through the same fine needle in one arm. The Haemonetics MCS+ system runs this as a cyclic draw–separate–return process.

How the session runs

Step by step.

Every part of the session is decided by the treating physician. Nothing is booked from a menu.

  1. 01

    Assessment & consent

    A full physician review with recent bloodwork, cardiac and coagulation screen. We discuss what plasma exchange can and cannot do, and confirm suitability. Nothing is booked from a menu.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    You rest in a private suite. One fine intravenous access is placed in a single arm — there is no second needle in the opposite arm as with older systems. Vital signs are monitored continuously.

  3. 03

    Cyclic apheresis

    The MCS+ draws a small volume of blood, separates plasma from your blood cells, discards the plasma, and returns your cells to you — then repeats. The whole cycle runs through the one needle. A session takes about two hours at most.

  4. 04

    Fluid replacement

    As plasma is removed, sterile albumin and saline are infused as replacement so your blood volume stays balanced throughout the session.

  5. 05

    Recovery & review

    Brief post-procedure observation, hydration, and a written summary. Follow-up bloodwork is scheduled to guide any further sessions.

Why it’s built this way

The advantages.

  • Single-needle, one-arm access

    One fine cannula in one arm for the whole session. Two-access systems typically require a needle in each arm and take three to four hours.

  • Up to two hours per session

    Cyclic operation on the MCS+ keeps the session short and comfortable, under continuous physician supervision.

  • Continuous physician oversight

    Every session runs in the Taipei clinic under a supervising physician — not delegated to a technician.

  • Lower circulating burden

    Removes a portion of plasma-borne inflammatory and metabolic factors; your own bloodwork is how we measure change, not the appearance of the collection bag.

Blood purification is offered only at the Taipei clinic. Photographs of the post-exchange collection bag are illustrative only — color and cloudiness are not measures of health. Laboratory results are the basis for assessment and follow-up.
